Team members share your account's links, domains and analytics. Open **Settings** in the sidebar and choose **Users** to see who has access.

![Team Members page](/docs/images/users.png)

## Invite a member

1. Click **Invite Member**.
2. Enter the person's **Name** and **Email Address**.
3. Pick a **Role**: **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer**. New invites default to **Member**.
4. Click **Send Invite**.

You'll see "Invitation sent to" followed by their email address, and they're added to the list.

The number of team members depends on your plan: 2 on Starter, 5 on Pro and 10 on Business, and unlimited on Enterprise. See [pricing](/pricing).

## Change a member's name or role

Click the **...** menu next to the member and choose **Edit**. Update the name or role and click **Save**. The email address can't be changed. To move someone to a different address, remove them and invite the new one.

## Remove a member

Click the **...** menu next to the member, choose **Remove**, then confirm with **Remove**. They lose access to the account right away, and this can't be undone.
